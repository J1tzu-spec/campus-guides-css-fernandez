Enrollment can be a stressful process, but with the right preparation, you can make it smooth and easy. Follow these steps to ensure a successful enrollment experience.

    1. Check the enrollment schedule and requirements.
    2. Prepare the necessary documents.
    3. Submit your enrollment form and pay the fees.
    4. Attend the orientation session.
    5. Get your student ID and other materials.
    6. Start your classes and enjoy your first semester!
